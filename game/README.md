# ⚔️ POKEYAMA

> Catch them. Train them. Battle them.
> And whatever you do… protect the snacks.

**Pokeyama** is an original creature-collecting and battling game universe filled with strange animals, elemental powers, ridiculous personalities, hidden regions, evolutions, rival trainers, and legendary creatures.

It started as a bedtime brainstorm between a mom and her seven-year-old daughter.

She was supposed to be going to sleep.

Instead, she invented monsters.

So naturally, we built a universe.

---

# 🌎 Welcome to Pokeyama

Far beyond the ordinary world lies **Pokeyama**, a wild land where creatures known as **Yamas** live among forests, mountains, volcanic islands, marshes, beaches, villages, and occasionally pantries.

Every Yama has its own:

* Elemental type
* Battle abilities
* Personality
* Habitat
* Strengths
* Weaknesses
* Special moves
* Evolutions
* Rare forms
* Backstory

Some Yamas are tiny and adorable.

Some are armored.

Some control water.

Some are literally on fire.

And some will fight you over a cracker.

# 🥚 The Original Six

The first known Yamas of the Pokeyama universe were discovered during one extremely productive bedtime.

---

# 001 — KAWAKIWI 🥝

### The Kiwi Bird Yama

**Type:** Nature / Wind
**Habitat:** Kiwi Woods
**Temperament:** Energetic
**Battle Style:** Speed / Air Attacks

Kawakiwi looks like a bird crossed with a kiwifruit.

Its fuzzy brown feathers make it nearly invisible among the strange fruit growing throughout Kiwi Woods.

The bright green feathers beneath its wings contain markings that look suspiciously like kiwi seeds.

Kawakiwi may look round and harmless, but it can move incredibly fast.

When threatened, it whips leaves and debris into powerful spiraling attacks before charging its opponent with its long beak.

### Battle Moves

* 🌿 Leaf Cyclone
* 🥝 Kiwi Kick
* 💨 Forest Dash
* 🪶 Feather Strike
* 🌪 Seed Storm

### Signature Move

**KIWI TEMPEST**

Kawakiwi spins at incredible speed, creating a storm of leaves, seeds, and wind before launching directly at its opponent.

---

# 002 — PUFFIPUPPI 🐶🐡

### The Puffer Puppy Yama

**Type:** Water
**Habitat:** Puffi Beach
**Temperament:** Playful
**Battle Style:** Defense / Water Attacks

Puffipuppi is a puppy with an unusual defense mechanism.

When frightened, excited, angry, hungry, confused, or mildly inconvenienced, Puffipuppi inflates into an enormous spiky ball.

Its body becomes nearly impossible to knock over.

Despite its round appearance, Puffipuppi can launch itself through the air with surprising speed.

Its favorite activity is chasing bubbles along the shoreline.

### Battle Moves

* 💦 Bubble Bark
* 🐾 Aqua Paw
* 🫧 Puppy Puff
* 🌊 Splash Charge
* 🐡 Spike Defense

### Signature Move

**TIDAL PUFF**

Puffipuppi inflates to maximum size before launching forward inside a spinning wave of water.

---

# 003 — POKIPONY 🦔🐴

### The Quill Pony Yama

**Type:** Earth / Armor
**Habitat:** Pokey Plains
**Temperament:** Brave
**Battle Style:** Defense / Charge

Pokipony is a powerful combination of pony, armadillo, and porcupine.

Its body is covered in overlapping armor plates while long quills protect its back, mane, and tail.

Normally friendly, Pokipony becomes extremely dangerous when threatened.

Its armor allows it to charge through rocks, fallen trees, and apparently anything else it decides should no longer be standing.

Approach hugs carefully.

### Battle Moves

* 🪨 Stone Charge
* 🦔 Quill Burst
* 🛡 Armor Curl
* 🐎 Stampede
* 💥 Ground Smash

### Signature Move

**QUILLQUAKE**

Pokipony charges forward with its quills raised and crashes into the ground, sending rocks and shockwaves across the battlefield.

---

# 004 — PANSHEE 🐼🍪

### The Pantry Panda Yama

**Type:** Snack / Fighting
**Habitat:** Pantry Village
**Temperament:** Sneaky
**Battle Style:** Trick Attacks / Close Combat

Panshee has one goal.

Snacks.

No pantry is safe.

Panshee possesses an almost supernatural ability to hear a snack cabinet opening from enormous distances.

While most people assume Panshee spends its time eating, experienced trainers know that Panshee has developed an entire fighting style based around food.

Crackers become throwing stars.

Cookies become shields.

And crumbs become distractions.

### Battle Moves

* 🍪 Snack Attack
* 🥨 Cracker Strike
* 🐾 Panda Punch
* 🍿 Pantry Raid
* ✨ Crumb Storm

### Signature Move

**MIDNIGHT MUNCH**

Panshee creates a spinning storm of snacks before launching a surprise attack from inside the chaos.

---

# 005 — TOYO TAPU 🐸💧

### The Marsh Mystic Yama

**Type:** Water / Mystic
**Habitat:** Tapu Marsh
**Temperament:** Wise
**Battle Style:** Magic / Water Control

Toyo Tapu is an ancient toad Yama found deep inside Tapu Marsh.

It is often seen carrying a twisted wooden staff containing a mysterious glowing water stone.

Toyo Tapu can manipulate the water around it, creating waves, shields, whirlpools, and powerful streams.

It rarely attacks first.

Mostly because it already knows how the battle is going to end.

Probably.

### Battle Moves

* 💧 Aqua Orb
* 🌊 Marsh Wave
* 🐸 Tapu Hop
* 🌀 Whirlpool
* 🌿 Lily Shield

### Signature Move

**TAPU TIDAL RING**

Toyo Tapu summons a massive circle of water around the battlefield and sends it crashing inward toward its opponent.

---

# 006 — FLAMIKA 🦩🔥

### The Flaming Flamingo Yama

**Type:** Fire / Air
**Habitat:** Flamingo Lagoon
**Temperament:** Dramatic
**Battle Style:** Fire / Speed

Flamika is a flamingo.

A flaming flamingo.

Its brilliant pink feathers burn without ever turning to ash.

When Flamika spreads its wings, flames sweep through the air behind it like ribbons.

Flamika is elegant.

Flamika is powerful.

Flamika knows both of these things.

### Battle Moves

* 🔥 Flame Feather
* 🦩 Flamingo Fury
* ☄️ Fire Dive
* 🌋 Heat Wave
* 🔥 Blazing Wing

### Signature Move

**FLAMING FLAMINGO**

Flamika spreads its wings and surrounds itself with fire before launching into a spinning aerial attack.

Yes.

That is the actual move name.

# 📖 THE STORY BEGINS

It was supposed to be bedtime.

The lights were off.

The blankets were tucked in.

Everything was quiet.

And then someone said:

> What if there was a bird that looked like a kiwi?

Somewhere beyond the edge of the ordinary world, something moved inside the trees.

A small brown creature stepped out of the leaves.

Its belly was bright green.

Its wings fluttered.

Its enormous eyes looked toward the path ahead.

Kawakiwi had awakened.

Then came a strange sound from the ocean.

A round puppy bounced out of the waves.

Puffipuppi.

Something thundered across the plains.

Pokipony.

A pantry door creaked open.

Panshee.

Deep within the marsh, two glowing eyes appeared beneath a lily pad.

Toyo Tapu.

And somewhere beyond the mountains, the night sky suddenly turned orange.

A flaming pink bird spread its wings above a volcano.

Flamika.

Six creatures.

Six regions.

And an entire world still waiting to be discovered.

**Pokeyama had begun.**
