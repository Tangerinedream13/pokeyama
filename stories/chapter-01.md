# Chapter 01: Awakening

It was supposed to be bedtime.

The lights were off.
The blankets were tucked in.
Everything was quiet.

Then someone whispered:

"What if there was a bird that looked like a kiwi?"

Somewhere beyond the ordinary world, leaves shifted in the dark.

A small brown creature stepped from the vines.
Its belly was bright green.
Its eyes reflected moonlight.

Kawakiwi had awakened.

From the ocean came the sound of splashing foam.
Puffipuppi bounced out of the surf.

Across the plains, stone cracked under heavy hooves.
Pokipony thundered past.

A pantry door creaked in the village hills.
Panshee smiled.

In the marsh, two glowing eyes appeared beneath a lily pad.
Toyo Tapu watched.

Beyond the mountains, the night sky glowed orange.
Flamika opened fire-bright wings above the lagoon.

Six Yamas.
Six regions.
One world beginning all at once.

Pokeyama had begun.
