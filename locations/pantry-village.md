# Pantry Village

## Region Type
Village hub / food market district

## Known Yama
- Panshee (#004)

## Region Notes
- Shops and kitchens make this the food crafting center.
- Nighttime increases trickster and snack-type encounters.
- Starter trial idea: recover stolen snacks before sunrise.

## Encounter Ideas
- Common: crumb scouts
- Rare: midnight raiders
