# Puffi Beach

## Region Type
Tropical shore / tide pools

## Known Yama
- Puffipuppi (#002)

## Region Notes
- Tides shift encounter areas every few in-game hours.
- Bubble caves can hide items and rare forms.
- Starter trial idea: survive the Foam Current challenge.

## Encounter Ideas
- Common: shell hoppers
- Rare: deep-tide drifters
