# Kiwi Woods

## Region Type
Dense forest / wind corridors

## Known Yama
- Kawakiwi (#001)

## Region Notes
- Fruit-heavy canopy with hidden branch paths.
- Fast-moving air currents can boost speed-based moves.
- Starter trial idea: complete the Leaf Run route.

## Encounter Ideas
- Common: seed critters, vine burrowers
- Rare: storm-wing variants
