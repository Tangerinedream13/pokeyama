# Tapu Marsh

## Region Type
Fog marsh / mystic wetlands

## Known Yama
- Toyo Tapu (#005)

## Region Notes
- Low visibility with rotating lily-pad pathways.
- Water and mystic moves gain terrain bonuses.
- Starter trial idea: cross the Whisper Pool without sinking.

## Encounter Ideas
- Common: marsh skippers
- Rare: rune toads
