# 🌙 Pokéyama

> A creature-collecting world invented at bedtime when we were definitely supposed to be sleeping.

Welcome to **Pokéyama**, a strange, adorable, occasionally spiky universe filled with creatures known as **Pokéyamas**.

Some are fluffy.

Some are pokey.

Some are on fire.

And some are just looking for snacks.

Pokéyama started as a bedtime brainstorm between a mom and her 7-year-old daughter and is now becoming its own creature-collection game and story universe.

---

## 🌎 The World of Pokéyama

Somewhere beyond the ordinary world lies **Pokéyama**, a land filled with unusual creatures hiding in forests, beaches, mountains, gardens, kitchens, pantries, and other mysterious places.

Every Pokéyama has its own:

- Species
- Type
- Personality
- Habitat
- Special abilities
- Favorite foods
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Story

Some Pokéyamas may evolve.

Some may have rare forms.

Some might become your best friend.

And at least one will probably steal your snacks.

## 🥝 Kawakiwi

<img src="images/kawakiwi.png" alt="Kawakiwi" width="96" />

**Creature:** Kiwi Bird
**Type:** Fruit / Flying
**Habitat:** Kiwi groves and shady forests

Kawakiwi is a little bird that looks suspiciously like a kiwifruit.

Its fuzzy brown feathers help it hide among fallen fruit, where it waits very quietly until someone walks by.

Then it runs.

Very fast.

- Kiwi Kick
- Fruit Roll

- Seed Storm

# 📖 The Story Begins...

It was supposed to be bedtime.

The lights were off.

The blankets were tucked in.

And then someone said:

> "What if there was a bird that looked like a kiwi?"

And somewhere, in a world that hadn't existed five minutes earlier, a tiny fuzzy bird opened its eyes.

Its name was Kawakiwi.

Soon there was a Puffipuppi.

Then a Pokipony.

A snack-hunting Panshee.

A mysterious Toyo Tapu.

And finally, a flaming flamingo named Flamika.

Then came Florzilla, a floral kaiju terror.

Munchee, a one-toothed beaver that loves wood and stumps.

Hapibuni, a glittery bunny idol with spinning ears.

And Capybasha, a capybara built for smashing.

Then Pengiro arrived, sliding in on ice and firing freezing blasts.

Then Lit zipped in, glowing bright and firing gooey light orbs.

Then Hoshiyuki arrived under the midnight sky, casting moonbeams across the stars.

Pokéyama had begun.
