# 🌙 Pokéyama

> A creature-collecting world invented at bedtime when we were definitely supposed to be sleeping.

Welcome to **Pokéyama**, a strange, adorable, occasionally spiky universe filled with creatures known as **Pokéyamas**.

Some are fluffy.

Some are pokey.

Some are on fire.

And some are just looking for snacks.

Pokéyama started as a bedtime brainstorm between a mom and her 7-year-old daughter and is now becoming its own creature-collection game and story universe.

---

## 🌎 The World of Pokéyama

Somewhere beyond the ordinary world lies **Pokéyama**, a land filled with unusual creatures hiding in forests, beaches, mountains, gardens, kitchens, pantries, and other mysterious places.

Every Pokéyama has its own:

- Species
- Type
- Personality
- Habitat
- Special abilities
- Favorite foods
- Strengths
- Weaknesses
- Story

Some Pokéyamas may evolve.

Some may have rare forms.

Some might become your best friend.

And at least one will probably steal your snacks.

## 🥝 Kawakiwi

<img src="images/kawakiwi.png" alt="Kawakiwi" width="96" />

**Creature:** Kiwi Bird
**Type:** Fruit / Flying
**Habitat:** Kiwi groves and shady forests

Kawakiwi is a little bird that looks suspiciously like a kiwifruit.

Its fuzzy brown feathers help it hide among fallen fruit, where it waits very quietly until someone walks by.

Then it runs.

Very fast.

### Possible Abilities

- Kiwi Kick
- Fruit Roll
- Fuzzy Feathers
- Seed Storm

---

## 🐡 Puffipuppi

<img src="images/puffipuppi.png" alt="Puffipuppi" width="96" />

**Creature:** Puffer Puppy
**Type:** Water / Fluff
**Habitat:** Beaches, tide pools, and anywhere comfortable

Puffipuppi is a round, chubby puppy with the unusual ability to puff itself up like a blowfish.

Usually Puffipuppi inflates when scared.

Sometimes it inflates because it is excited.

Sometimes it just does it.

### Possible Abilities

- Puppy Puff
- Bubble Bark
- Belly Bounce
- Mega Floof

---

## 🦔 Pokipony

<img src="images/pokipony.png" alt="Pokipony" width="96" />

**Creature:** Quill Pony
**Type:** Earth / Pokey
**Habitat:** Grasslands and rocky trails

Pokipony looks like a pony crossed with a very determined armadillo.

Its back is covered in protective quills that stand straight up whenever Pokipony senses danger.

Pokipony loves running but does not recommend hugging from behind.

### Possible Abilities

- Quill Charge
- Pokey Gallop
- Armor Curl
- Spike Shake

---

## 🐼 Panshee

<img src="images/panshee.png" alt="Panshee" width="96" />

**Creature:** Pantry Panda
**Type:** Snack
**Habitat:** Kitchens, pantries, snack cabinets

Panshee is a panda with one mission:

**Find snacks.**

It has an incredible ability to detect the opening of a pantry door from almost anywhere.

No one knows exactly how.

### Possible Abilities

- Snack Attack
- Pantry Raid
- Cookie Crunch
- Midnight Munch

### Favorite Foods

Pretty much everything.

## 🐸 Toyo Tapu

<img src="images/toyo-tapu.png" alt="Toyo Tapu" width="96" />

**Creature:** Toad
**Type:** Water / Mud
**Habitat:** Ponds, streams, and muddy places

Toyo Tapu is a mysterious little toad that spends most of its time sitting very still and looking like it knows something you don't.

It loves puddles, rainstorms, and surprising travelers by suddenly jumping enormous distances.

### Possible Abilities

- Tapu Hop
- Mud Splash
- Lily Launch
- Super Croak

---

## 🦩🔥 Flamika

<img src="images/flamika.png" alt="Flamika" width="96" />

**Creature:** Flaming Flamingo
**Type:** Fire / Flying
**Habitat:** Hot springs, volcano lakes, and tropical beaches

Flamika is a flamingo.

Who is flaming.

Literally.

Its bright pink feathers glow with heat, and when Flamika gets excited, tiny flames appear along the edges of its wings.

Flamika is elegant, dramatic, and extremely aware of both facts.

### Possible Abilities

- Flamingo Flame
- Fire Feather
- Blazing Beak
- Hot Step

# 📖 The Story Begins...

It was supposed to be bedtime.

The lights were off.

The blankets were tucked in.

And then someone said:

> "What if there was a bird that looked like a kiwi?"

And somewhere, in a world that hadn't existed five minutes earlier, a tiny fuzzy bird opened its eyes.

Its name was Kawakiwi.

Soon there was a Puffipuppi.

Then a Pokipony.

A snack-hunting Panshee.

A mysterious Toyo Tapu.

And finally, a flaming flamingo named Flamika.

Pokéyama had begun.
