# 012 - LIT

## The Glowy Firefly Yama

Type: Light / Bug
Habitat: Moonlit forests, wetlands, and lantern caves
Temperament: Mischievous
Battle Style: Ranged Glow / Status Control

## Description

Lit is a tiny firefly Pokeyama that glows brighter when excited. It shoots sticky glowy goo and floating light orbs that can blind, slow, or trap opponents.

## Battle Moves

- Glow Goo
- Orb Shot
- Flash Drift
- Lumen Sting

## Signature Move

NOVA SWARM

Lit releases a spiral of glowing orbs, then detonates them in a burst of blinding light and sparkling goo.
