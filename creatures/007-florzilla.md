# 007 - FLORZILLA

## The Floral Kaiju Yama

Type: Nature / Bloom
Habitat: Volcano gardens, overgrown ruins, and moonlit greenhouses

Florzilla is a giant lizard-like Pokeyama covered in blooming flowers and thorny petals. It stomps through the world like a monster movie nightmare, except the nightmare smells like a garden.
