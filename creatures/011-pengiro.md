# 011 - PENGIRO

## The Ice Blaster Penguin Yama

Type: Ice / Water
Habitat: Frozen coves, glacier cliffs, and snowstorm shores
Temperament: Focused
Battle Style: Ranged Ice / Slide Control

## Description

Pengiro is a determined penguin Pokeyama that blasts streams of freezing ice from its beak. It glides across frozen ground at high speed, then locks down opponents with sharp ice shots.

## Battle Moves

- Frost Shot
- Ice Beam Peck
- Glacier Slide
- Snow Burst

## Signature Move

POLAR CANNON

Pengiro charges a compressed sphere of arctic energy and fires a piercing blast that freezes the battlefield in its wake.
