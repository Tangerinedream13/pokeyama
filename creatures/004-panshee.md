# 004 - PANSHEE

## The Pantry Panda Yama

Type: Snack / Fighting
Habitat: Pantry Village
Temperament: Sneaky
Battle Style: Trick Attacks / Close Combat

## Description

Panshee can detect snacks from impossible distances and turns crumbs, crackers, and cookies into full battle tactics.

## Battle Moves

- Snack Attack
- Cracker Strike
- Panda Punch
- Pantry Raid
- Crumb Storm

## Signature Move

MIDNIGHT MUNCH

Panshee creates a spinning snack storm and disappears into it before striking.
