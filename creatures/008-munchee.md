# 008 - MUNCHEE

## The Big-Tooth Beaver Yama

Type: Wood / Bite
Habitat: Riverbanks, dam forests, and stump piles
Temperament: Hungry
Battle Style: Chop / Build

## Description

Munchee is a beaver with one huge tooth and an even bigger appetite for wood. It loves chewing logs, gnawing tree stumps, and turning fallen branches into surprisingly sturdy dams.

## Battle Moves

- Timber Bite
- Stump Smash
- Log Jam
- Dam Dash

## Signature Move

WILDBITE WORKSHOP

Munchee chomps down with its giant tooth and sends splinters flying before building a blocking dam out of the remains.
