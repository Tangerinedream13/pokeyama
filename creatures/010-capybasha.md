# 010 - CAPYBASHA

## The Smash Capybara Yama

Type: Earth / Bash
Habitat: Mud flats, riverbanks, and training grounds
Temperament: Determined
Battle Style: Heavy Impact / Tank

## Description

Capybasha is a huge, strong capybara Pokeyama built for smashing through obstacles. It may look calm for a second, but once it starts charging, everything in its path gets flattened.

## Battle Moves

- Bash Charge
- Mud Slam
- Capy Crash
- Heavy Roll

## Signature Move

RIVER RAMPART

Capybasha launches its massive body forward in a crushing attack that leaves a wall of mud and broken ground behind.
